01

EMS Station Location Optimization

Project Lead · 120急救站点选址优化

Leading a six-person team to improve emergency medical station placement across Shenzhen. We combined road-network features with 360K real Tencent Maps ETA samples to train an XGBoost travel-time model, reaching MAE 1.64 min and R² 0.863. Finalist in the 8th China University Mechanical Engineering Innovation & Creativity Competition.
